### Background
FHF funded AKVAFAGJUS (project 901972) because suitable localities are scarce and production ambitions often stall on site applications. The report also follows MILJØREG (FHF 901738) on environmental regulation and coexistence, and ran in parallel with sister project ÅRSAK (FHF 901995). Combined results from the two projects are to appear in a separate short report. SALT 1110 is dated 22 May 2026 (139 pages). Authors are Guri Hjallen Eriksen, Henrik Hareide and Tale Skrove. SALT led chapters 2, 3 and 5. Aqknowledge led chapter 4.

### Methods
The team built a dataset of 251 refusal cases from 2020-2025: full or partial county refusals of new sites, biomass or area changes, species changes and purpose changes, plus supporting sector decisions and appeals. 149 cases come from FAKS. The rest were collected because FAKS was incomplete and not updated for all of 2024. Co-location cases are excluded. Most cases are salmonid sea sites (192), then cod (51). Methods mix quantitative sorting of refusal grounds with qualitative aquaculture and legal reading. ChatGPT was used as support for quality-assuring assessments. The legal chapter sampled 98 of 149 FAKS cases (only 10 of 50 from Trøndelag), about 20 appeal decisions, and six granted permits. The authors call this combination new and say it was adjusted during the work. Not every theme was analysed in depth.

### Main findings
Some decisions have clear refusal grounds. In others facts, professional assessment and discretion run together, so the decisive ground is hard to name. Some decisions are not site-specific and instead use general statements about possible harm and uncertainty. Precaution is applied differently across cases and authorities. The authors say it is not meant to be applied for safety's sake.

Mattilsynet is the most frequent refuser (142 recorded, 137 after later changes). Lice infection risk is the largest coded Mattilsynet ground (68). After the 1 September 2024 reorganisation, 32 first-instance refusals were issued in 2025, and the appeal unit upheld all 18 cases decided in that window. Historical lice-limit breaches, including the 2024 marine heatwave, are often treated as proof of weak internal control.

County Aquaculture Act refusals include 14 coastal-cod cases and five wild-salmonid cases. Six of the 14 sit in a mapped spawning field or area. For marine species the locality application is also a company-biomass application. Pollution-law refusals turn on recipient capacity (50 coded cases, 29 in Nordland) and nature. Coral and sponge are the largest nature theme (21). Hard-bottom coral-forest density thresholds in use range from 1-2 to more than 10 colonies per 100 m2, which can change whether a survey is read as forest or scattered colonies. Seabird refusals (seven in the qualitative set) often rest on general disturbance and kelp-food-web uncertainty rather than site-specific population effect.

Political county bodies can refuse after all sector authorities have granted, or grant after the administration has refused. FAKS templates are voluntary. From 2 February 2024 the general discharge-permit duty was replaced by standardised environmental requirements, but much of the studied practice is still under the older Pollution Control Act.

Provisional recommendations include more first-instance dialogue, shared guidance and templates, written precaution reasons, a public decision database, and lawyer involvement early. County political weighings under the Aquaculture Act should meet a high standard of reasons.

### Uncertainty and limitations
Trend statements should be read with caution. Table 7 (refusals by county and authority) and figures 1-14 are images in the extract. Table 7 cells were not readable. The legal sample under-represents Trøndelag. One author is disclosed as having worked for aquaculture parties in several studied cases. Institute risk reports are described as diagnoses, not operational gap-closing plans. Genetic risk from farmed to wild cod is said to rest on potential for effect more than documented introgression.

### Relevance to this corpus
This is the refusal-practice layer of locality access: how sector vetoes and county discretion actually stop or reshape applications, including lice, coral, seabird and coastal-cod grounds.

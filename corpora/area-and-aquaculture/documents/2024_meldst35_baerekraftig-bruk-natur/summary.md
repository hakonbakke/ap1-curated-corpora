### Background
Meld. St. 35 (2023-2024) is the government's white paper to the Storting on sustainable use and conservation of nature. It is Norway's action plan for biological diversity, following up the global Kunming-Montreal nature agreement of December 2022. It sets direction for nature management on land and at sea. The stated purpose is not to list every measure or to have all answers.

### Methods
Government policy document. It builds on the previous action plan in Meld. St. Natur for livet (2016). Process and involvement are described in chapter 2.7. This curation walked the table of contents, the sammendrag (chapter 1), the policy emphasis in chapter 2, status for Norwegian nature including sea and coast (3.2.1), the fisheries and aquaculture sector (5.5.2), and the aquaculture subsection under sustainable management of areas used for agriculture, aquaculture, fisheries and forestry (around the traffic-light discussion). Many figures and most land chapters were not walked as data.

### Main findings
Area change on land and at sea is named as the largest negative driver of nature loss in Norway, even though Norwegian nature is described as in better condition than in many other countries. Other drivers are overharvest, climate, pollution and alien species. The sum of small, necessary or desirable actions can exceed what nature can tolerate.

The government wants both sustainable use and conservation. Nature underpins prosperity, ecosystem services, and district livelihoods. Tools emphasised include regular overviews, a nature account (naturregnskap), menus of measures for ecosystems, a target to reduce development of especially important nature areas, and principles for sustainable area management. The nature agreement's 30 percent area goal is named, including protection and other effective area-based measures. The sea still has remaining area toward that goal in the sammendrag's wording.

Norway already has nationwide area management through the Planning and Building Act and ocean and water management plans, plus the Nature Diversity Act beside sector laws.

Fisheries, hunting and aquaculture are presented as value creation and food production that depend on functioning ecosystems. Norway is among the countries that produce most seafood, mostly for export. Aquaculture covers several species and forms in sea, on land and in freshwater, with different effects on nature, environment and landscape. Central instruments are area planning, permits and direct regulation of establishment, operation and closure. A main goal is greatest possible value creation within sustainable frames. NOU 2023:23 is cited. The government will consider this further in an upcoming white paper on aquaculture.

Under area management of aquaculture, municipal plans with impact assessment are described as important for use versus protection and for locating aquaculture areas. The government wants better guidance on coastal-zone planning and to look at the frames for planning aquaculture.

The traffic-light system (from 2016) divides the coast into 13 production areas. The only environmental indicator at the time of writing is mortality of out-migrating salmon smolts from salmon lice. Red (over 30 percent probable lice-induced smolt mortality) can cut permit capacity by 6 percent. An expert group on the quality standard for wild salmon concluded that traffic-light thresholds and the quality standard do not steer toward the same goal. Yellow light and no capacity change can still breach the quality standard when natural marine survival is moderate to low. The group said a fundamental review of aquaculture management is needed to care for wild salmon. Sea trout is planned into the traffic-light colouring from 2026. Cod farming in spawning areas and spawning fields was clarified as prohibited in a 2024 regulation change.

### Uncertainty and limitations
This is a government action plan, not an independent empirical study. Many chapters and figures were not walked. Claims about upcoming aquaculture white paper and 2026 sea-trout traffic-light inclusion are government intentions, not results.

### Relevance to this corpus
This is the national land-and-sea nature-allocation text. Aquaculture is one sector inside a larger policy of use and conservation. It also states that traffic-light lice cuts and the wild-salmon quality standard do not share a goal.
